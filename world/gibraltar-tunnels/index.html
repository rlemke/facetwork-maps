The Hollow Mountain

Gibraltar holds about 55 km of tunnels inside a 6.7 km² rock, cut between 1782 and 1967. Almost all of it is military and sealed. This is what OpenStreetMap knows about it — and what it does not.

The missing 48.4 km is not an error. It is classified, sealed, or both — the limit of what a public map can hold.

OSM holds 12% of the tunnels and 2.26× the official road length. Both are definitional, not sloppy: one counts what cannot be surveyed, the other counts service ways and both carriageways that no road statistic does.
