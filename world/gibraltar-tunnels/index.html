The Hollow Mountain

Gibraltar holds about 55 km of tunnels inside a 6.7 km² rock, cut between 1782 and 1967. Almost all of it is military and sealed. This is what OpenStreetMap knows about it — and what it does not.

Recorded

  • Tunnelling ran 1782–99, 1933–38, 1939–45 and 1956–68.
  • Excavation peaked in 1942 at over 35,000 cubic yards; the spoil extended the airfield.
  • The Stay Behind Cave, cut in 1941–42 for six men to be sealed inside, stayed a rumour until 1997.
