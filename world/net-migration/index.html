About this data

Net migration is immigrants minus emigrants: shaded green where more people arrive than leave (net immigration) and red where more leave than arrive (net emigration), per 1,000 population. Drag the year slider (1960–2025) or press play; click a country for its numbers and history.

Source: World Bank — Net migration (SM.POP.NETM) and Total population (SP.POP.TOTL), derived from the UN World Population Prospects. Values are estimates; ~65 years is the longest openly-available global migration series (consistent per-country data does not exist for the early 20th century).
