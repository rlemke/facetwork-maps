What the columns mean

Value
the group's notional value at the last snapshot (closed groups: at the close).
P/L, P/L %
profit or loss on the money put in, in dollars and as a percent of it.
Benchmark
how much the benchmark index fund (SPY unless stated) moved over the same days.
vs benchmark
the group's P/L % minus the benchmark's. Positive means the picks beat holding the index.
Planned end
the end of the holding period the group was opened with. A group stays open, and is marked every weekday, until someone closes it.

Paper trading only, not investment advice. These are notional (simulated) positions recorded to measure a selection strategy. No orders were placed and no money moved. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Past performance does not predict future results, and the scores are a ranking over historical factors, not a forecast: the benchmark column is there to check whether the strategy beat simply holding the index.

Prices come from a delayed, unofficial feed (Yahoo Finance through yfinance) and can be wrong. Figures exclude commissions, spreads, slippage, taxes and dividends.
