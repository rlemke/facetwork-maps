Scenic and historic roads from OpenStreetMap. Toggle each layer with the checkboxes; click a dot, or anywhere along a route, for its details. Each layer unions the DESIGNATED routes (route relations carrying a network tag - US state scenic-byway networks, US:NHT National Historic Trail auto-tour routes, US:auto_trail) with the per-way tags scenic=yes and historic=*. Coverage is crowd-sourced and very uneven BETWEEN CONTINENTS: the scenic-byway network convention is essentially North American (OSM has no scenic-road route type at scale - route=scenic_route is 3 relations worldwide), while roman_road and hollow_way are essentially European. A road absent from a layer is untagged, not un-scenic and not un-historic. Geometry is simplified by about 33 m, under one pixel at these zooms.
