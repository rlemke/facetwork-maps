Roads maps

Scenic and historic roads from OpenStreetMap, one map per region, with an independent checkbox for each layer. Each layer unions the DESIGNATED routes - which OSM models as route relations carrying a network tag (US state scenic-byway networks, US:NHT National Historic Trail auto-tour routes, US:auto_trail) - with the per-way tags scenic=yes and historic=*. The designated routes carry roughly 10x the scenic road-km and 26x the historic road-km that the per-way tags do in North America. Coverage is crowd-sourced and very uneven BETWEEN CONTINENTS, which these maps show rather than hide: the scenic-byway convention is essentially North American (OSM has no scenic-road route type at scale - route=scenic_route is 3 relations worldwide), while roman_road and hollow_way are essentially European. A road missing from a layer is untagged, not un-scenic and not un-historic.
