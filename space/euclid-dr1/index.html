Euclid's first data release on the sky

Every object in ESA Euclid's DR1 wide-survey catalogue, counted on the sky as you would see it from Earth: drag to look around, scroll to zoom. The coloured patches are where Euclid has looked, and their colour is how many galaxies it found there.

Colour the survey by

Zoomed in past the map's 3-arcminute cells: the colour is hidden; zoom out to see it. Galaxy markers and outlines may not draw closer than about 1 degree.
