Plan ahead

Two days before
Make the three sauces and the rubs; the sauces taste better after a day. Freeze the paletas.
The day before
Rub the pork shoulder and ribs. Make the coleslaw dressing, the cauliflower salad and the key lime pie. Hard-boil the eggs.
Early morning
Start the pork shoulder: it needs 8–10 hours at low heat. The ribs go on about 5 hours before serving.
An hour before
Grill the vegetables and fill the eggs. Let the pork rest wrapped while the chicken thighs go on the hot side of the grill.
Serving
Pull the pork and set the sauces out in bowls so guests choose their region. Keep the cold sides in the shade or over ice.
