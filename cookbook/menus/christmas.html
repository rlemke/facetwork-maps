Plan ahead

A week before
Bake the cookies; vanillekipferl and speculoos keep two weeks in a tin. Order the roast: allow about one rib for two people.
The day before
Salt the roast and leave it uncovered in the fridge overnight, which dries the surface for a better crust. Make the eggnog so it has a night to chill.
Morning
Take the roast out two hours before it goes in. Bake the focaccia and the pudding; both reheat.
While the roast cooks
Make the celeriac mash and the creamed spinach, and dress the salad leaves just before sitting down.
While the roast rests
Roast the sprouts at high heat and warm the pudding. Rest the beef at least 20 minutes before carving.
