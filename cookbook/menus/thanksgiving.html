Plan ahead

3–4 days before
Thaw a frozen turkey in the fridge, allowing about a day per 2 kg (4–5 lb). Make the cranberry sauce; it keeps a week.
The day before
Bake the pies and the cornbread. Make the stuffing up to the point of baking and refrigerate it. Hard-boil and fill the eggs. Trim the green beans and sprouts.
Morning
Take the turkey out of the fridge an hour before roasting and work out its finishing time: it needs to rest 30–45 minutes, which is when the oven is free for the sides.
While the turkey roasts
Assemble the green bean casserole. Cook and mash the cauliflower; it reheats well.
While the turkey rests
Raise the oven to roast the sprouts and bake the stuffing and casserole together. Make the gravy from the drippings. Whip the cream last.
