Deviled eggs

Hard-boiled egg halves filled with a tangy yolk, mayonnaise and mustard mixture and dusted with paprika.

Dairy-free 0.4 g net carbs Serves 12 · 2 halves prep 20 min · cook 12 min · rest 15 min

Ingredients

  • 12 large eggs
  • ⅓ cup mayonnaise (sugar-free, avocado or olive oil) (73 g)
  • 2 tsp prepared mustard (yellow or Dijon) (10 g)
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • ¼ tsp fine salt
  • ⅛ tsp ground black pepper
  • ½ tsp hot pepper sauce, optional
  • ½ tsp sweet or smoked paprika, to finish
  • 1 tbsp fresh chives, chopped

Nothing here is a low-carb stand-in, so a conventional version would be much the same.

Contains: eggs.

Method

  1. Put the eggs in a saucepan, cover with cold water by 2.5 cm (1 in) and bring to a boil. Cover, take off the heat and leave 11 minutes.
  2. Move the eggs to a bowl of iced water for 15 minutes, then peel under running water.
  3. Halve the eggs lengthwise and pop the yolks into a bowl. Mash with a fork until fine, then stir in the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, salt, pepper and hot sauce until smooth.
  4. Spoon or pipe the filling into the whites. Dust with paprika and scatter with chives. Serve within a few hours, kept chilled.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • Eggs a week or so old peel more cleanly than very fresh ones.

On the menu for: Thanksgiving dinner, Outdoor barbecue.

Where it comes from

Stuffed eggs · Rome · Southern Europe · ancient

Boiled eggs served with spiced sauces were eaten in ancient Rome, and recipes for stuffed eggs with the yolk mixed with seasonings appear in medieval cookbooks, including one from 13th-century Andalusia. The word 'devilled', for food made hot with mustard or pepper, appears in English in the late 1700s, and in the United States 'deviled eggs' became a party and picnic staple in the twentieth century.
