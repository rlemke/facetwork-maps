Vegan crêpes

Thin, flexible egg-free crêpes held together with flaxseed and psyllium, for folding around sweet or savoury fillings; vegan as written.

Dairy-free Vegan 2.0 g net carbs Serves 4 · 2 crêpes prep 5 min · cook 25 min · rest 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1¼ cups unsweetened almond milk (300 g)
  • 3 tbsp golden flaxseed meal (21 g)
  • ¼ cup almond flour (blanched, finely ground) (24 g)
  • 1 tbsp coconut flour
  • 2 tsp psyllium husk powder
  • ⅛ tsp fine salt
  • 2 tbsp dairy-free butter (stick, plant-based) (28 g), melted, plus extra for the pan

Method

  1. Blend all the ingredients until completely smooth, about 45 seconds.
  2. Rest the batter 15 minutes so the flax and psyllium gel; it thickens noticeably. It should flow like thin cream, so blend in water a tablespoon at a time if it is thicker.
  3. Heat a 20 cm (8 in) non-stick pan over medium heat and brush lightly with vegan butter.
  4. Pour in 4 tablespoons of batter and tilt and swirl at once to coat the base thinly and evenly.
  5. Cook 1½–2 minutes, without moving it, until the surface looks matt and dry and the edge lifts and is lightly browned. Loosen all round with a thin spatula, flip and cook 30 seconds more.
  6. Stack on a plate and repeat, stirring the batter each time and thinning it with a little water if it thickens as it stands.

Tips

  • Without egg there is nothing to set the crêpe quickly, so patience is the method: wait until the top is fully dry before flipping, or it tears. The first one is usually a test.
  • They are a little thicker, nuttier and speckled compared with egg crêpes, and they stay pliable when cold, so they fold and roll well for wraps.
  • Fill with coconut yogurt and berries, or with sautéed mushrooms and spinach for a savoury version.

Where it comes from

Crêpe · Quimper, Brittany · Western Europe · medieval

Brittany is the home of the crêpe: buckwheat spread there in the late Middle Ages, thin buckwheat galettes became a staple, and sweeter wheat crêpes followed as white flour became affordable. Traditional Breton galettes are often made with little or no egg, so egg-free crêpes are not a modern novelty. Flax- and psyllium-bound versions like this one are a recent, grain-free adaptation from low-carb and vegan home cooking.
