Almond-flour sandwich loaf

A sliceable, sturdy sandwich loaf from almond flour and psyllium that toasts well.

Dairy-free 2.6 g net carbs Serves 14 · 1 slice prep 15 min · cook 1 h 10 min · rest 1 h

Ingredients

  • 2½ cups almond flour (blanched, finely ground) (240 g)
  • ½ cup psyllium husk powder (72 g)
  • 2 tbsp coconut flour (14 g)
  • 3 tsp baking powder (14 g)
  • 1½ tsp fine salt
  • 5 large egg whites
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice (15 g)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
  • 1½ cups water (356 g), boiling

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F) and line a 23 × 13 cm (9 × 5 in) loaf pan with parchment.
  2. Whisk the almond flour, psyllium, coconut flour, baking powder and salt in a large bowl.
  3. Add the egg whites, egg, lemon juice and olive oil and stir briefly, then pour in the boiling water and mix vigorously until it forms a smooth, thick dough.
  4. With wet hands, shape into a log and press into the pan, smoothing the top. Score a line down the middle.
  5. Bake 65–75 minutes, until deep golden and hollow-sounding when tapped underneath. If the top browns early, cover loosely with foil.
  6. Cool completely in the pan, at least 1 hour, before slicing; cut warm, the crumb is gummy.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • Psyllium gives this dough a bread-like chew; brands differ, and some darken the crumb purple-grey — it is harmless.
  • Slice the whole loaf and freeze it; toast slices straight from frozen.

Where it comes from

Sandwich loaf · Chillicothe, Missouri · North America · 1928

The tall, square-sided pan loaf became the standard sandwich bread with industrial baking. Pre-sliced bread was first sold commercially in 1928 by a bakery in Chillicothe, Missouri, using Otto Rohwedder's slicing machine. This grain-free loaf is a modern low-carb imitation of that everyday bread.
