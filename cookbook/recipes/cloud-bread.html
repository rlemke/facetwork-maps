Cloud bread

Feather-light rounds of whipped egg white and cream cheese — a modern low-carb invention for sandwiches.

Dairy-free option 0.7 g net carbs Serves 6 · 1 round prep 10 min · cook 30 min · rest 10 min

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs, separated
  • ¼ tsp cream of tartar
  • 45 g full-fat cream cheese, softened
  • ⅛ tsp fine salt
  • ¼ tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 150 °C (300 °F) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Whip the egg whites with the cream of tartar to stiff, glossy peaks.
  3. In another bowl, beat the yolks, cream cheese, salt and baking powder until smooth.
  4. Fold a third of the whites into the yolk mixture to lighten it, then fold in the rest gently, keeping as much air as you can.
  5. Spoon into 6 rounds about 1.5 cm (½ in) thick on the sheet.
  6. Bake 25–30 minutes, until golden and firm to a light touch. Cool on the sheet; they firm and become flexible as they rest.

Dairy-free version

  • Use dairy-free cream cheese (cashew or almond based) instead of full-fat cream cheese.

Dairy-free cream cheese makes a looser yolk mixture, so the rounds spread more and bake flatter; fold very gently and bake a few minutes longer.

Tips

  • Store in a bag with the rounds separated by parchment; they turn soft and bread-like overnight.

Where it comes from

Cloud bread · United States · North America · 2000s

Cloud bread is a modern invention of low-carb home cooks, circulating in the United States from the 2000s and later popular on social media. It imitates soft sandwich bread using whipped egg whites for lift; there is no traditional dish behind it.
