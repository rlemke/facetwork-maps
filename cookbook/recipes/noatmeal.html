"Noatmeal" hot cereal

A warm, porridge-like bowl of hemp hearts, chia and flax simmered in almond milk, with cinnamon and vanilla.

Dairy-free 4.5 g net carbs Serves 2 · 1 bowl prep 5 min · cook 5 min · rest 3 min

Ingredients

  • ⅓ cup hemp hearts (53 g)
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds (12 g)
  • 2 tbsp golden flaxseed meal (14 g)
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened shredded coconut (10 g)
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk (240 g)
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ⅛ tsp fine salt
  • 2 tsp granulated erythritol
  • ½ tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp almond butter, unsalted (16 g), to serve

Method

  1. Put the hemp hearts, chia, flaxseed meal, coconut, almond milk, cinnamon and salt in a small saucepan.
  2. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring, and cook 3–4 minutes until thickened.
  3. Take off the heat, stir in the erythritol and vanilla and let stand 2–3 minutes; it keeps thickening.
  4. Divide between two bowls and top each with a spoonful of almond butter.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • Loosen with a splash of almond milk if it sets too thick.
  • Mix a double batch of the dry ingredients and keep it in a jar for quick mornings.

Where it comes from

Porridge · Scotland · Northern Europe · medieval

Grain porridges are eaten almost everywhere, but oat porridge is especially associated with Scotland, where oats grew well in the cool, wet climate and were a staple for centuries. This grain-free version imitates the texture with seeds that thicken as they absorb liquid.
