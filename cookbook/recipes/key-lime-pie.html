Key lime pie

A sharp, pale lime custard set in a cinnamon-almond crust, with a homemade coconut condensed milk taking the place of the canned kind; dairy-free as written.

Dairy-free 4.9 g net carbs Serves 10 · 1 slice prep 30 min · cook 45 min · rest 4 h

Ingredients

Crust

  • 1½ cups almond flour (blanched, finely ground) (144 g)
  • 4 tbsp coconut oil (54 g), melted
  • 2 tbsp granulated erythritol (24 g)
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ⅛ tsp fine salt

Coconut condensed milk

  • 1½ cups coconut cream (canned, unsweetened) (360 g)
  • ½ cup allulose (96 g)

Filling

  • 4 large egg yolks
  • ½ cup fresh lime juice (120 g), Key limes if you can find them
  • ⅛ tsp fine salt

Method

  1. Simmer the coconut cream and allulose in a wide saucepan over medium-low heat, stirring often, for 20–25 minutes, until reduced to about 1 cup and thick enough to coat a spoon. Cool to lukewarm.
  2. Heat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F). Stir the crust ingredients together, press them into a 23 cm (9 in) pie plate and bake 10 minutes. Lower the oven to 160 °C (325 °F).
  3. Whisk the yolks until pale, then whisk in the cooled condensed coconut, followed by the lime juice and salt. It will thicken as the acid meets the yolks.
  4. Pour into the crust and bake 15 minutes, just until the surface is set but still pale.
  5. Cool for 30 minutes, then refrigerate at least 4 hours before slicing.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • Do not boil the coconut cream hard; a gentle simmer keeps it from splitting into oil.
  • Persian limes are fine; the filling will just be a touch less floral than with Key limes.

Where it comes from

Key lime pie · Key West, Florida · North America · late 1800s

Key limes grew in the Florida Keys, where fresh milk was scarce before refrigeration and the arrival of a mainland road. Canned sweetened condensed milk, available from the later 1800s, made a custard that sets with lime juice alone. Who first made the pie is unknown, and the stories naming an inventor are local legend; Florida made it the official state pie in 2006.
