Peanut butter fat bombs

Salty-sweet peanut butter cups set in the freezer, with a thin cap of sugar-free dark chocolate.

Dairy-free 2.2 g net carbs Serves 12 · 1 fat bomb prep 15 min · cook 5 min · rest 1 h

Ingredients

Peanut layer

  • ½ cup peanut butter, smooth, unsweetened (128 g)
  • ¼ cup coconut oil (54 g)
  • 2 tbsp powdered erythritol (confectioners' style) (16 g)
  • ½ tsp pure vanilla extract
  • ⅛ tsp fine salt

Chocolate top

  • 40 g sugar-free dark chocolate chips (stevia or erythritol sweetened)
  • 1 tsp coconut oil

Not low-carb is a rough estimate of a conventional version, not a tested recipe: powdered sugar for powdered erythritol (confectioners' style) (0.7 × by weight); semisweet chocolate for sugar-free dark chocolate chips (stevia or erythritol sweetened). Everything else is as written. Sweeteners are matched for sweetness, and ordinary recipes are often sweeter, so the real difference is usually larger.

Contains: coconut, peanuts.

From typical product labels, which vary by brand: powdered erythritol (confectioners' style), sugar-free dark chocolate chips (stevia or erythritol sweetened).

⚠️ Many brands contain milk fat; choose one labelled dairy-free.

Method

  1. Line a 12-cup mini-muffin tin with paper cases, or use silicone moulds.
  2. Warm the peanut butter and the ¼ cup coconut oil in a small pan over low heat, stirring, until smooth and pourable. Whisk in the powdered erythritol, vanilla and salt.
  3. Spoon into the cases, filling each about two-thirds full, and freeze for 20 minutes until the surface is firm.
  4. Melt the chocolate chips with the teaspoon of coconut oil in short bursts in the microwave or in a bowl over barely simmering water, stirring until smooth.
  5. Spoon a thin layer of chocolate over each cup, tilting to cover the peanut layer, and freeze at least 30 minutes more.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • If your peanut butter is unsalted, add a few flakes of salt on the chocolate before it sets.

Where it comes from

Fat bomb · United States · North America · 2010s

Fat bombs are a modern invention of the low-carb and ketogenic diet community in the United States, spread largely through blogs and forums in the 2010s. This version imitates the American peanut butter cup, a candy that dates to the early twentieth century, but replaces the sugar with erythritol and much of the bulk with coconut oil.
