Peppermint patties

Cool, firm peppermint fondant discs dipped in dark chocolate; dairy-free as written.

Dairy-free 1.2 g net carbs Serves 16 · 1 patty prep 40 min · cook 5 min · rest 1 h 30 min

Ingredients

Filling

  • ¼ cup coconut oil (54 g), softened, not melted
  • 2 tbsp coconut cream (canned, unsweetened) (30 g)
  • 1 cup powdered erythritol (confectioners' style) (120 g)
  • 1 tsp peppermint extract
  • 0.0625 tsp fine salt

Coating

  • 120 g sugar-free dark chocolate chips (stevia or erythritol sweetened)
  • 45 g unsweetened baking chocolate (100% cacao)
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil (14 g)

Not low-carb is a rough estimate of a conventional version, not a tested recipe: powdered sugar for powdered erythritol (confectioners' style) (0.7 × by weight); semisweet chocolate for sugar-free dark chocolate chips (stevia or erythritol sweetened). Everything else is as written. Sweeteners are matched for sweetness, and ordinary recipes are often sweeter, so the real difference is usually larger.

Contains: coconut.

From typical product labels, which vary by brand: coconut cream (canned, unsweetened), peppermint extract, powdered erythritol (confectioners' style), sugar-free dark chocolate chips (stevia or erythritol sweetened), unsweetened baking chocolate (100% cacao).

⚠️ Many brands contain milk fat; choose one labelled dairy-free.

Method

  1. Beat the softened coconut oil and coconut cream until smooth, then beat in the powdered erythritol, peppermint and salt to make a stiff, smooth paste. Taste and add more peppermint if you like.
  2. Roll into 16 balls, set on a parchment-lined tray and flatten each into a disc about 4 cm (1.5 in) across. Freeze 30 minutes, until very firm.
  3. Melt both chocolates with the coconut oil over barely simmering water. Cool until just warm.
  4. Dip each frozen disc in the chocolate with a fork, let the excess drip off, and set back on the parchment. Refrigerate until set, about 1 hour. Keep chilled: coconut oil softens above about 24 °C (76 °F).

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • Check the chocolate chips are dairy-free: many sugar-free brands contain milk fat.

Where it comes from

Peppermint patty · York, Pennsylvania · North America · 1940

The chocolate-covered peppermint patty is best known from the York Peppermint Pattie, first made in York, Pennsylvania, in 1940. Peppermint creams covered in chocolate were made by confectioners before then, but the firm, flat patty became the American standard.
