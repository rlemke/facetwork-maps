Italian olive-oil cake

A moist, golden single-layer cake made with fruity olive oil and orange zest, dairy-free as written.

Dairy-free 2.7 g net carbs Serves 10 · 1 slice prep 15 min · cook 40 min · rest 30 min

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs, at room temperature
  • ⅔ cup granulated erythritol (129 g)
  • 1 tbsp orange zest
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • ½ cup olive oil (108 g), extra-virgin, fruity rather than peppery
  • ⅓ cup unsweetened almond milk (79 g)
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 2 cups almond flour (blanched, finely ground) (192 g)
  • 2 tbsp coconut flour (14 g)
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp fine salt
  • 1 tbsp powdered erythritol (confectioners' style), for dusting

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F). Oil a 23 cm (9 in) round pan and line the base with parchment.
  2. Whisk the eggs, erythritol and zests with an electric mixer for 3–4 minutes, until thick and pale.
  3. With the mixer on low, pour in the olive oil in a thin stream, then the almond milk and vanilla.
  4. Whisk the almond flour, coconut flour, baking powder and salt together and fold them in until just combined.
  5. Pour into the pan and bake 35–40 minutes, until golden and a skewer comes out clean.
  6. Cool 15 minutes in the pan, then turn out onto a rack. Dust with powdered erythritol before serving.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • The oil's flavour comes through, so taste it first: a harsh oil makes a bitter cake.

Where it comes from

Oil cake · Italy · Southern Europe · by the 1900s

In the olive-growing regions of Italy, olive oil has long stood in for butter in home baking, and simple oil-based cakes flavoured with citrus are made from Liguria to Sicily. There is no single recorded origin; the cake is a family of regional home recipes rather than a named invention. This version is dairy-free as written.
