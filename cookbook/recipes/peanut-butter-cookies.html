Peanut butter cookies

Flourless peanut butter cookies with the classic fork crosshatch, dairy-free as written and ready in twenty minutes.

Dairy-free 2.8 g net carbs Serves 16 · 1 cookie prep 10 min · cook 11 min · rest 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup peanut butter, smooth, unsweetened (256 g), well stirred
  • ½ cup granulated erythritol (96 g)
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ tsp fine salt, skip if your peanut butter is salted

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Stir all the ingredients together until thick and smooth.
  3. Roll into 16 balls and set 5 cm (2 in) apart. Press each flat with a fork, then press again at right angles to make a crosshatch.
  4. Bake 10–12 minutes, until the edges are just golden. The cookies are very soft when hot: leave them on the sheet for 15 minutes before moving.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • Use a no-stir-style peanut butter with only peanuts and salt; a very oily natural one makes the cookies spread.
  • Dip the fork in erythritol between cookies so it doesn't stick.

Where it comes from

Peanut butter cookie · United States · North America · early 1900s

Peanut butter became a common American food around the turn of the twentieth century, and peanut butter cookies were printed in US cookbooks in the early 1900s. By the 1930s the fork-pressed crosshatch, which flattens the stiff dough so it bakes evenly, had become the standard way to shape them.
