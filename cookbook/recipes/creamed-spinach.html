Creamed spinach

The steakhouse side: wilted spinach bound in a garlicky cream sauce with a little parmesan and nutmeg.

Dairy-free option 5.7 g net carbs Serves 6 · about 1/2 cup prep 15 min · cook 15 min

Ingredients

  • 900 g fresh spinach, tough stems removed
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter (28 g)
  • 40 g onion, very finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¾ cup heavy whipping cream (178 g)
  • 115 g full-fat cream cheese, softened
  • ¼ cup grated parmesan (25 g)
  • ⅛ tsp ground nutmeg
  • ½ tsp fine salt
  • ¼ tsp ground black pepper

Nothing here is a low-carb stand-in, so a conventional version would be much the same.

Contains: dairy. Dairy-free version contains: coconut, tree nuts.

Method

  1. Wilt the spinach in batches in a large pot with a splash of water over medium-high heat, 2–3 minutes per batch. Drain in a colander.
  2. When cool enough to handle, squeeze the spinach hard in a clean towel to remove as much water as you can, then chop it coarsely.
  3. Melt the butter in the dry pot over medium heat and cook the onion for 3–4 minutes until soft. Add the garlic for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the cream and cream cheese and whisk until smooth, then simmer 2–3 minutes until slightly thickened.
  5. Stir in the spinach, parmesan, nutmeg, salt and pepper and cook 2 minutes, until hot and creamy. Serve straight away.

Dairy-free version

  • Use dairy-free butter (stick, plant-based) instead of unsalted butter.
  • Use full-fat coconut milk (canned) instead of heavy whipping cream.
  • Use dairy-free cream cheese (cashew or almond based) instead of full-fat cream cheese.
  • Use nutritional yeast flakes instead of grated parmesan (0.5 × the amount).

Coconut milk and dairy-free cream cheese give a looser, slightly sweeter sauce; nutritional yeast stands in for the parmesan's savouriness. Simmer a minute longer to thicken it.

Tips

  • Squeezing the spinach well is the whole trick; leftover water turns the sauce thin and grey-green.

On the menu for: Christmas dinner.

Where it comes from

Creamed spinach · New York City · North America · 1900s

Spinach cooked with cream or a white sauce is an old European preparation, found in French and English cookery of the 1800s. In the United States it became a fixture of the twentieth-century steakhouse menu, served alongside steaks and chops, and New York steakhouses did much to make it the standard pairing.
