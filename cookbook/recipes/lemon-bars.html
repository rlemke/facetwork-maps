Lemon bars

A sharp, silky lemon custard baked over a crisp almond shortbread base, dairy-free as written.

Dairy-free 1.6 g net carbs Serves 16 · 1 square prep 20 min · cook 40 min · rest 3 h

Ingredients

Crust

  • 1½ cups almond flour (blanched, finely ground) (144 g)
  • 3 tbsp granulated erythritol (36 g)
  • ⅛ tsp fine salt
  • ¼ cup coconut oil (54 g), melted

Filling

  • 4 large eggs
  • ½ cup granulated erythritol (96 g)
  • 2 tbsp allulose (24 g)
  • ½ cup fresh lemon juice (122 g), from about 3 lemons
  • 1 tbsp lemon zest
  • 2 tbsp coconut flour (14 g)
  • ⅛ tsp fine salt

To finish

  • 1 tbsp powdered erythritol (confectioners' style)

Everything else is as written. Sweeteners are matched for sweetness, and ordinary recipes are often sweeter, so the real difference is usually larger.

Contains: coconut, eggs, tree nuts.

From typical product labels, which vary by brand: allulose, coconut flour, granulated erythritol, powdered erythritol (confectioners' style).

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F). Line a 20 cm (8 in) square pan with parchment, leaving an overhang.
  2. Mix the crust ingredients, press evenly into the pan and bake 12–15 minutes, until golden. Lower the oven to 160 °C (325 °F).
  3. Whisk the eggs, erythritol and allulose until smooth. Whisk in the lemon juice, zest, coconut flour and salt, then leave 5 minutes for the coconut flour to hydrate and whisk again.
  4. Pour over the warm crust and bake 20–25 minutes, until the filling is set at the edges with only a slight wobble in the centre.
  5. Cool to room temperature, then chill at least 3 hours. Dust with powdered erythritol and cut into 16 squares with a clean, wet knife.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • Strain the filling for the smoothest top; coconut flour can leave a few specks.

Where it comes from

Lemon bar · United States · North America · mid-1900s

Lemon bars, also called lemon squares, became common in American community cookbooks in the mid-twentieth century. They descend from older English lemon curd and lemon tarts, pressed into a sheet pan with a shortbread base so they could be cut into squares.
