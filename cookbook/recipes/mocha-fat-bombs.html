Mocha fat bombs

Dark, coffee-strong chocolate squares made with cocoa butter so they hold their shape a little longer out of the cold.

Dairy-free 1.2 g net carbs Serves 12 · 1 fat bomb prep 15 min · cook 5 min · rest 1 h

Ingredients

  • 40 g cocoa butter, chopped
  • ⅓ cup coconut oil (72 g)
  • ¼ cup almond butter, unsalted (64 g)
  • 3 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder (16 g)
  • 2 tsp instant espresso powder
  • 3 tbsp powdered erythritol (confectioners' style) (24 g)
  • 2 tbsp coconut cream (canned, unsweetened) (30 g)
  • ½ tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 0.0625 tsp fine salt

Method

  1. Melt the cocoa butter in a heatproof bowl over a pan of barely simmering water, then add the coconut oil and almond butter and stir until smooth.
  2. Take the bowl off the heat. Whisk in the cocoa, espresso powder and powdered erythritol until the espresso has dissolved.
  3. Whisk in the coconut cream, vanilla and salt. The mixture will thicken slightly.
  4. Pour into 12 silicone moulds or a small loaf tin lined with parchment, about 10 × 15 cm (4 × 6 in).
  5. Freeze for 1 hour until hard. If you used a tin, lift out and cut into 12 squares with a warm knife.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • Cocoa butter sets firmer than coconut oil; if you have none, use the same weight of extra coconut oil and keep the bombs frozen.

Where it comes from

Fat bomb · United States · North America · 2010s

Like other fat bombs, this is a modern low-carb creation from American ketogenic-diet home cooks of the 2010s. The mocha flavour, coffee with chocolate, takes its name from the Yemeni port of Mocha, once a major coffee-trading centre, but the sweet itself has no traditional ancestor.
