Raspberry sorbet

Bright, seedless raspberry sorbet with a little lemon; nothing in it but fruit, water and sweetener.

Dairy-free 4.2 g net carbs Serves 6 · 1/2 cup prep 15 min · cook 5 min · rest 6 h

Ingredients

  • 450 g raspberries, fresh or frozen
  • ¾ cup water (178 g)
  • ½ cup allulose (96 g)
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice (15 g)
  • 0.0625 tsp fine salt

Method

  1. Warm the water and allulose until dissolved, then cool.
  2. Blend the raspberries with the syrup, lemon juice and salt until smooth.
  3. Press through a fine sieve with the back of a spoon to remove the seeds.
  4. Refrigerate until very cold, about 2 hours, then churn in an ice-cream maker. Freeze 3–4 hours before serving.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • With no machine, freeze in a shallow dish and blend the frozen chunks in a food processor until smooth, then refreeze briefly.

Where it comes from

Sorbet · Naples · Southern Europe · 1600s

The word sorbet comes, through Italian sorbetto, from the Turkish şerbet and Arabic sharbat, sweet cooling drinks of the Islamic world. Frozen versions made with ice and salt were developed in Italy in the seventeenth century and quickly taken up in France, where fruit sorbets became a fixture of fine dining.
