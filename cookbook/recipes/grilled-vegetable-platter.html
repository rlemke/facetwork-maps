Grilled vegetable platter

Zucchini, summer squash, eggplant, red pepper and red onion grilled until charred and soft, then dressed with garlic, herbs and olive oil.

Dairy-free 5.7 g net carbs Serves 8 · about 1 cup prep 20 min · cook 25 min · rest 10 min

Ingredients

  • 2 zucchini, sliced lengthwise 1 cm (1/2 in) thick
  • 2 yellow summer squashes, sliced lengthwise 1 cm (1/2 in) thick
  • 1 eggplant, sliced into 1 cm (1/2 in) rounds
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into wide strips
  • 110 g onion, red onion, cut into wedges through the root
  • ¼ cup olive oil (54 g), for brushing
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper

Dressing

  • 3 tbsp olive oil (40 g)
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar (15 g)
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ¼ cup fresh basil leaves, torn
  • 2 tbsp fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped

Nothing here is a low-carb stand-in, so a conventional version would be much the same.

Contains: none of the common allergen groups.

Method

  1. Heat a grill to medium-high, about 200–230 °C (400–450 °F), and clean and oil the grates. Brush the vegetables on both sides with oil and season with the salt and pepper.
  2. Grill in batches with the lid closed: zucchini and squash 3–4 minutes a side, eggplant 4–5 minutes a side, pepper and onion 5–6 minutes a side, until marked and tender. Oven alternative: roast on two oiled sheet pans at 220 °C (425 °F) for 20–25 minutes, turning once.
  3. Whisk the dressing oil, vinegar, garlic and oregano together.
  4. Arrange the vegetables on a platter, spoon the dressing over and rest 10 minutes so they soak it up. Scatter with basil and parsley. Serve warm or at room temperature.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • Eggplant drinks oil; brush it rather than pouring, and it will still soften fully on the grill.

On the menu for: Outdoor barbecue.

Where it comes from

Grilled vegetables (verdure grigliate) · Italy · Southern Europe · 1800s

Grilling vegetables over coals is an old Mediterranean habit, but this mix is relatively recent: peppers and summer squash came from the Americas after 1500, and the zucchini as we know it was developed in Italy around the 1800s. Platters of grilled vegetables dressed with oil and herbs are now a standard antipasto and side across Italy and the wider Mediterranean.
