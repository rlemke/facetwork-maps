Roasted garlic asparagus

Asparagus roasted hot with olive oil and garlic, finished with lemon zest, juice and flaky salt.

Dairy-free 2.7 g net carbs Serves 4 · 6 spears prep 5 min · cook 12 min

Ingredients

  • 24 asparagus spears, woody ends snapped off
  • 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • ¼ tsp fine salt
  • ¼ tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice (15 g)
  • ¼ tsp flaky sea salt, to finish

Nothing here is a low-carb stand-in, so a conventional version would be much the same.

Contains: none of the common allergen groups.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220 °C (425 °F). Toss the asparagus with the oil, salt and pepper on a rimmed sheet pan and spread in one layer.
  2. Roast 6 minutes, then scatter the garlic over, toss and roast 4–6 minutes more, until the spears are tender-crisp and lightly browned and the garlic is golden. Thick spears need the longer time.
  3. Move to a platter, grate over the lemon zest, sprinkle with the juice and flaky salt, and serve.

Dairy-free as written

Tips

  • Add the garlic partway through: sliced garlic burns and turns bitter if it roasts for the full time.

On the menu for: Family Sunday dinner.

Where it comes from

Asparagus · Rome · Southern Europe · ancient

Asparagus was cultivated and prized in ancient Rome; the Roman writer Cato gave instructions for growing it. Its short spring season made it a traditional Easter and spring vegetable across Europe, celebrated especially in Germany's white-asparagus season. Simply roasting it with oil and garlic is a modern home method.
