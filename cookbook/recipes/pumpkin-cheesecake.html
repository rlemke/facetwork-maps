Pumpkin cheesecake

A spiced pumpkin cheesecake on a pecan crust, the American autumn pie and cheesecake rolled into one.

Dairy-free option 5.6 g net carbs Serves 12 · 1 slice prep 30 min · cook 1 h 15 min · rest 8 h

Ingredients

Crust

  • 1 cup pecans (109 g), finely ground
  • ½ cup almond flour (blanched, finely ground) (48 g)
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter (57 g), melted
  • 2 tbsp granulated erythritol (24 g)
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon

Filling

  • 680 g full-fat cream cheese, at room temperature
  • ¾ cup granulated erythritol (144 g)
  • 1 cup pumpkin purée (canned, unsweetened) (245 g)
  • 4 large eggs
  • ¼ cup sour cream (58 g)
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¾ tsp ground ginger
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • ⅛ tsp ground cloves
  • ¼ tsp fine salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 160 °C (325 °F). Line the base of a 23 cm (9 in) springform pan with parchment.
  2. Mix the crust ingredients, press firmly over the base and bake 10 minutes. Let it cool.
  3. Beat the cream cheese and erythritol on low speed until smooth. Beat in the pumpkin, then the eggs one at a time, then the sour cream, vanilla, spices and salt, mixing only until combined.
  4. Pour onto the crust. Wrap the outside of the pan in foil, set it in a roasting tin and pour in hot water to come halfway up the side.
  5. Bake 65–75 minutes, until the edge is set and the centre wobbles slightly. Turn off the oven and leave the cake inside with the door ajar for an hour.
  6. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate at least 8 hours before slicing.

Dairy-free version

  • Use coconut oil instead of unsalted butter (0.85 × the amount) — melted.
  • Use dairy-free cream cheese (cashew or almond based) instead of full-fat cream cheese — block-style.
  • Use unsweetened coconut yogurt instead of sour cream.

The pumpkin helps the dairy-free version hold together well, but it is softer; chill it overnight and slice with a hot knife.

Tips

  • Blot the pumpkin purée between paper towels if it looks wet; excess water makes the filling weep.

Where it comes from

Cheesecake · New England · North America · late 1900s

Pumpkin pie has been an American autumn dessert since colonial New England and a Thanksgiving fixture since the nineteenth century. Combining its spiced pumpkin custard with cream-cheese cheesecake is a twentieth-century American idea, and by the late 1900s pumpkin cheesecake was a common holiday alternative to the pie.
