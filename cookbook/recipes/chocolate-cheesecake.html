Chocolate cheesecake

A dense, dark chocolate cheesecake on a cocoa-almond crust, baked slowly in a water bath.

Dairy-free option 5.8 g net carbs Serves 16 · 1 thin slice prep 30 min · cook 1 h 15 min · rest 8 h

Ingredients

Crust

  • 1¼ cups almond flour (blanched, finely ground) (120 g)
  • 3 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder (16 g)
  • 3 tbsp granulated erythritol (36 g)
  • 5 tbsp unsalted butter (71 g), melted
  • ⅛ tsp fine salt

Filling

  • 115 g unsweetened baking chocolate (100% cacao), chopped
  • 900 g full-fat cream cheese, at room temperature
  • ¾ cup granulated erythritol (144 g)
  • ¼ cup powdered erythritol (confectioners' style) (30 g)
  • 3 tbsp Dutch-process cocoa powder (16 g)
  • ½ cup sour cream (115 g)
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp instant espresso powder
  • ¼ tsp fine salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 160 °C (325 °F). Line the base of a 23 cm (9 in) springform pan with parchment.
  2. Mix the crust ingredients, press firmly into the pan and bake 10 minutes. Let it cool.
  3. Melt the chocolate gently over barely simmering water and let it cool until just warm.
  4. Beat the cream cheese, both sweeteners and the cocoa on low speed until completely smooth. Beat in the warm chocolate, then the sour cream.
  5. Add the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla, espresso powder and salt, mixing on low only until combined.
  6. Pour onto the crust, wrap the outside of the pan in foil and set it in a roasting tin. Pour in hot water to come halfway up the side.
  7. Bake 65–75 minutes, until the edge is set and the centre wobbles slightly. Turn off the oven and leave the cake inside with the door ajar for an hour.
  8. Cool, then refrigerate at least 8 hours before slicing.

Dairy-free version

  • Use coconut oil instead of unsalted butter (0.85 × the amount) — melted.
  • Use dairy-free cream cheese (cashew or almond based) instead of full-fat cream cheese — block-style.
  • Use unsweetened coconut yogurt instead of sour cream.

Chocolate hides the coconut and cashew flavours well, and the melted chocolate helps the dairy-free version set firmer than most.

Tips

  • Cut it into thin slices with a hot, dry knife; it is very rich.
  • Make sure the chocolate is only warm when added; hot chocolate scrambles the eggs, cold chocolate seizes into flecks.

Where it comes from

Cheesecake · New York City · North America · 1900s

Chocolate cheesecake is a twentieth-century American variation on the cream-cheese cheesecake that grew up around New York delicatessens. There is no single recorded inventor; flavoured cheesecakes became common in American bakeries and restaurants through the second half of the century.
