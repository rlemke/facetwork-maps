Dinner menus

Sets of the book's recipes for a gathering: what to serve together, how much a full plate adds up to, and a plan for getting it all on the table at once.

Holiday · serves 10–12

Thanksgiving dinner

The full American spread: a roast turkey with pan gravy, the casseroles and the cranberry sauce, then two pies. Every dish that is usually bread, potato or sugar has a low-carb stand-in, so the table looks the way it always does.

43.6 g net carbs for a plate of everything · about 106.4 g if made the ordinary way

 Holiday · serves 8

Christmas dinner

A standing rib roast with horseradish sauce, or a glazed ham, served with winter vegetables in the British and northern European style, and a cookie plate with the eggnog.

38.6 g net carbs for a plate of everything · about 97.1 g if made the ordinary way

 Gathering · serves 6

Family Sunday dinner

An easy, crowd-pleasing dinner for a family table: roast chicken, or a meatloaf for the children, with the comfort sides and a warm crumble.

32.2 g net carbs for a plate of everything · about 78.8 g if made the ordinary way

 Summer · serves 12–15

Outdoor barbecue

A backyard cookout across America's barbecue regions: Carolina pulled pork, Memphis ribs and grilled chicken, with three regional sauces, the classic cold sides and frozen desserts for a hot day.

34.2 g net carbs for a plate of everything · about 77.6 g if made the ordinary way

A “plate of everything” is one serving of every dish on the menu, dessert included, leaving out the alternatives marked or. It is a generous holiday plate; most people take less.
